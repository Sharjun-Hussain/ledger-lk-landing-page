Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 October 2026 • Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Inzeedo (PVT) Ltd ("we", "us") collects, uses, protects and deletes information when you use LedgerLK, our mobile bookkeeping and credit-ledger app for small businesses in Sri Lanka.

1. Who we are

LedgerLK is built and operated by Inzeedo (PVT) Ltd, based in Sainthamaruthu / Colombo, Sri Lanka. We are the data controller for information processed through the app. Contact us at hello@inzeedo.lk or support@inzeedo.lk.

2. Data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account information — your name, email address (Google sign-in) or phone number (phone OTP login).
  • Business records you enter — shop details, customer names and contact numbers, credit ledger entries, cash in / cash out records, due dates, receipt photos and reminder history.
  • Support messages — emails you send us, so we can help and improve the app.
  • Basic technical data — app version, Android version and crash logs, used only to fix bugs and keep the app stable.

Your customers never need the app: payment reminders reach them by WhatsApp or SMS, and we process only the contact details you provide for that purpose.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the app: ledgers, cash tracking, reminders, backups and multi-device sync.
  • To keep your account secure and recover it when you switch phones.
  • To send payment reminders you ask us to send.
  • To provide support and fix technical issues.
  • To improve LedgerLK using aggregated, non-identifying statistics.

We do not use your business data for advertising, and we never sell your data to anyone.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We share data only with the services required to operate LedgerLK — secure cloud hosting and backup providers, and WhatsApp/SMS delivery partners for the reminders you send. These providers may process data only on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.

We disclose information only if required by Sri Lankan law or a valid court order, and we will notify you unless legally prohibited.

5. Security

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored on access-controlled cloud infrastructure with regular backups. Google sign-in and phone OTP mean we never store app passwords. No system is perfectly secure, but we review and strengthen our safeguards continuously and will notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

6. Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active so your ledgers stay synced across devices. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app via Settings > Delete Account, or through our account-deletion page. Once confirmed, your data is purged from our live systems and backups within 7 days.

7. Your rights

You may request a copy or export of your data, correct inaccurate information, or delete your account and data at any time. PDF exports and monthly statements are also available directly inside the app. To exercise any right, email support@inzeedo.lk — we respond to all requests.

8. Children

LedgerLK is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of LedgerLK after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about privacy, your data, or deletion? Reach us at support@inzeedo.lk or hello@inzeedo.lk — Inzeedo (PVT) Ltd, Sainthamaruthu / Colombo, Sri Lanka.
