3. Plans & payments

  • Free (Rs. 0 forever) — 1 shop, up to 50 customers, credit ledgers, cash tracking, 10 reminders/month. The Free plan includes ads.
  • Pro (Rs. 499/month or Rs. 4,990/year) — up to 100 customers, 150 WhatsApp/SMS reminders/month, no ads, receipt photos, PDF exports, cloud backup & multi-device sync, priority support.
  • Business (Rs. 999/month or Rs. 9,990/year) — everything in Pro plus unlimited customers, up to 5 shops, staff accounts with roles, 400 SMS/month included, SMS add-on packages available, advanced reports & analytics, VIP support.

Prices are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR), payable by bank transfer or card. Yearly plans include 2 months free. You can cancel anytime — paid plans stay active until the end of the billing period and are not refunded mid-cycle. We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice; changes never apply retroactively.
